How the rehearsal works

One meeting. One bounded action.

  1. 01

    Show the release evidence

    Show a release checklist and the isolated target side by side. Explain what was observed and what is still unknown.

  2. 02

    Approve one replay

    Review the target and action. An explicit human decision authorizes one replay in the isolated fixture, not production.

  3. 03

    Check the result before retrying

    Simulate a lost acknowledgement. Hold the outcome as unknown; independently verify whether the action took effect.

Proposed measures: time to evidence, approval-to-result time, independent readbacks, and duplicate effects. No combined measurements yet.
